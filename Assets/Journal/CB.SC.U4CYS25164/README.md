1. Gratitude Towards Sreekanth

2. Gratitude Towards the Canteen Worker

   Visiting the canteen during my classes was common. From the busy crowd of students heading for their meals in the canteen, the most notable figure was the Malayali woman. Sharing the same original ethnic group as the woman in the canteen was noticeable; even though the interaction was short,

Whenever I got in a line to order my food, she managed to see me and smiled as she took my order despite the location of my spot in the back of the line. To begin with, I did not know why she did that, but later on, I figured it was due to the fact that I always ensured that when I got served by the lady with my food, I was sure to express my gratitude by saying “thank you.”

I am deeply thankful to her for this simple gesture of kindness on her part. Her actions showed that even the smallest gesture can foster feelings of gratitude, respect, and human connection that are not easily forgotten. Now each time I go to the canteen, I am reminded that even the smallest gesture can go a long way in fostering human connection.

3. Gratitude Towards My Hostel Warden
   I have experienced a situation that could have easily resulted in a stressful disaster for me. For my break, I was scheduled to go back home. I already booked my train ticket in advance; however, during my application for my hostel outpass, I mistakenly marked the wrong time in my application form. I got worried when I saw my mistake and could not change my application request. I could not leave campus without approval.
   
   Being nervous about approaching my hostel warden to brief them about the current situation and explain to them why I wanted to obtain the outpass, anticipating that perhaps the authorities would get frustrated with me for my carelessness! To my surprise, instead of any of that frustration and scolding for my carelessness once again, wardedn patiently understood the current situation and told me to apply for an outpass once again. He promised to sanction an outpass for me immediately – done! No issues whatsoever!

   I am sincerely thankful to my warden for showing me such kindness during the situation rather than showing strictness towards me. It shows me how kindness is an integral part of showing authority, rather than strictness, and how it’s still possible to do the right thing even when it comes to issues where one may have acted mistakenly.
